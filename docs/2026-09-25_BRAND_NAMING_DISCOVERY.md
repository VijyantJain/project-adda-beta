# Project Adda — final brand naming discovery sprint

Date: 2026-09-25. Decision D055. STATUS: DISCOVERY STARTED, NO FINAL NAME CHOSEN, NO NAME/DOMAIN/TRADEMARK CLEARED, NO APP CODE OR DATA CHANGES.

## Brief — confirmed existing product truths
- Adda is a WORKING internal label, never assume it will be final app brand or buy Adda domains.
- Product: real existing friends and private Crews; interactive Drops, real reveals and conversation; a guaranteed solo Daily Aura loop independent of Crew; earned 3-star titles; v0.8 permanent evolving purple/magenta/cyan Aura Orb with genuine earned-level visual progression; no fake social proof, synthetic mate activities or generic infinite public feed as current promise.
- Distinct brand name (final still open) vs named product mechanics such as Aura, Crew, Drop, Charge, Orb and achievement earned titles; do not automatically rename any of those unless founder chooses. Orb is visual signature, not necessarily app name.
- India-first current testers but broader future-language/geography potential to clarify. No age-target inference from the separate fresh global product project; Project Adda has its own audience discovery.
- Brand style should allow warm social personality PLUS futuristic luminous identity; not merely a gamification/rewards/crypto name; adult-friendly if future audience expands, easy invite copy: 'Join my Crew on ____', 'I sent you a Drop on ____'.

## Naming exploration territories, NOT selections
1. Human connection / belonging: a welcoming place for actual friends, durable if features evolve beyond playful Drops.
2. Energy / orbit / radiance: luminous evolving Orb and cumulative earned Aura without implying AI diagnosis, crypto tokens or horoscope.
3. Playful conversation / curiosity: speakable invite-friendly coined language; avoid tying brand only to polling/quiz.
4. Hybrid original coined names: combine belonging with movement/light; require pronunciation and conflict review, not a random gibberish dump.

## Founder preferences to obtain BEFORE formal candidate ranking
A. Intended primary launch markets/languages and whether international pronunciation is non-negotiable.
B. Audience age breadth and brand maturity (playful youth vs broad adults vs balanced).
C. Word style (real word, invented, Indian-language-root with global delivery, or open); preferred 2–3 syllables; exclusions/cultural sensitivities.
D. Orb relation to product name (literal light/orbit/energy, subtle metaphor, completely independent visual icon).
E. Need exact .com versus acceptable .in/alternative TLD and annual first-year/renewal budget; no assumption about availability.
F. Emotional promise/tagline and brand personality; 3 brands liked and disliked by founder if available.

No empty web search is proof of availability or legal clearance.
- DO NOT claim current domain availability from non-transactional search. Don't purchase/reserve/change DNS without founder action. Keep Netlify preview, main, legacy keys and all scores untouched.
